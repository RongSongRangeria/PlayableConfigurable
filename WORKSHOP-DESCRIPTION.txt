[h1]PlayableConfigurable[/h1]

Makes races and subraces universally configurable and playable adapting to
[b]your[/b] mod list, every time you launch. Add or remove race mods and this
just keeps working (i hope): no need for conflicting patches and playable mods that cause conflict with your anim and model suites.

[b]Languages:[/b] English, Russian, Spanish, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional),
German, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Ukrainian, Polish. Follows your
Kenshi client automatically, with a manual override button in the window -
see Features below for details.

[h1]Requirements[/h1]
[b]Requires RE_Kenshi[/b] (v0.3.0+, tested against the current v0.3.4). Without
it this mod does nothing.
[url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kenshi/mods/847]Get RE_Kenshi here[/url] - just grab the latest release. It was built in RE_Kenshi 3.4, but theoretically it may work in 3.0.

[h1]How it works[/h1]
[list]
[*] Enable this mod and RE_Kenshi, then launch.
[*] Click the RACES button on the main menu.
[*] Toggle whole race groups or individual subraces. Presets: Enable All,
Disable All, Default All (whatever your mods themselves mark playable),
Vanilla Only (what base game kenshi has marked as playable).
[*] Changes apply immediately - no restart needed to see them in a new game.
[/list]

[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*] Rows are split into Race Groups, Modded Groups, and Uncategorized (races
with no group, sorted by whichever mod added them), so a big modlist doesn't
turn into a wall of checkboxes.
[*] Hover any entry to see which mod it's from, or that it's vanilla.
[*] Allow animal races to be selected (i don't promise all of these work well... i'm looking at you ironsides).
[*] Unlock per-start race restrictions.
[*] Saved memory for a race whose mod isn't currently loaded are remembered and
come back the moment that mod is re-enabled.
[*] Follows your Kenshi's own language automatically - 12 languages supported
(English, Russian, Spanish, Chinese Simplified & Traditional, German, French,
Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Ukrainian, Polish), with a Language button at
the bottom of the window if you want to override it manually. Only your
Kenshi's language and English are guaranteed to render correctly; the others
depend on what fonts are loaded and may show blanks instead of text.
[/list]

i made this partly because i was getting tilted trying to get new races and
animals from mods playable, and spent 3 hours launching and relaunching my
game trying to fix missing models. this was a side-project of that because
the conflicts were caused by 7 playable races mods.

order prioritization and solve my issue separately from other playables mods.
